FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my data used for training?

Not on Team or Enterprise plans. On Free and Grove you can opt out any time in Settings → Privacy.

Can Heartwood make mistakes?

Yes. Like any model it can be confidently wrong, so always verify important outputs.

Does it remember our conversations?

Only if Memory is on. You can view, edit and delete memories at any time.

Is Heartwood conscious?

No. Heartwood is a statistical model of language. It doesn’t have feelings, though it’s designed to be warm.

How do I cancel?

Settings → Billing → Cancel plan. No calls, no guilt trips.

Who builds Heartwood?

A team of 140 researchers and engineers across Lisbon and Toronto. We’re hiring.
